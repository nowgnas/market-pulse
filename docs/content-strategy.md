# Market Pulse Content Strategy

## Positioning

Market Pulse should be a market explanation site, not a high-frequency news rewrite feed.

The core promise is:

> Fewer updates, more context. One useful market explanation should help a busy reader understand what moved, why it mattered, what could be wrong, and what to check next.

## Publishing Rhythm

- Weekdays: publish one daily market insight.
- Saturday: publish one weekly review.
- Sunday: publish one week-ahead guide.
- Avoid publishing multiple short posts in the same day unless there is a clear editorial reason.

## Article Standard

Every daily insight should include:

- One core market driver.
- Two or three concrete evidence points from market data or linked news.
- A plain-language explanation of why the driver matters.
- A counter-scenario or risk to the main interpretation.
- A reader checklist with observable data points.
- Source notes that distinguish market data from news interpretation.

Do not publish if the article only:

- Repeats index moves.
- Lists headlines without interpretation.
- Uses unsupported exact numbers.
- Uses exaggerated claims such as "historic", "explosive", or "record-breaking" without explicit source evidence.
- Gives investment advice or implies a guaranteed direction.

## Evergreen Guides

Build durable guide content alongside daily posts. Good guide topics include:

- How to read KOSPI with exchange rates and foreign flows.
- Why U.S. rates affect Korean growth stocks.
- How semiconductor news affects Korean market sentiment.
- What to check during earnings season.
- A morning, intraday, and closing market routine for busy readers.

These guides should be written to remain useful for months, not hours.

## Trust Requirements

The site should clearly disclose:

- AI is used to draft and structure posts.
- The site publishes only when content passes basic quality checks.
- Data comes from public market data and linked news sources.
- Content is informational and not investment advice.
- Users can contact the operator about corrections or concerns.

## AdSense Review Gate

Do not request review until:

- At least 30 source-backed daily insights or evergreen guides are live.
- The latest 10 posts are free of unsupported numbers and exaggerated claims.
- At least 5 evergreen guides are live or scheduled.
- The homepage and about page clearly explain the site's editorial standards.
- Search Console no longer shows old low-quality URLs as indexed.
